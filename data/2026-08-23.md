### [1] [Stream4D: 4D-Consistency for Streaming Autoregressive Diffusion Video Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19556)
*Yuanhao Ban,Jiaqi Feng,Hengguang Zhou,Xiaohuan Pei,Justin Cui,Cho-Jui Hsieh*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出Stream4D，用前馈式4D重建奖励替代静态3D重建批评器，为流式自回归扩散视频生成模型提供动态场景一致性监督，并结合运动先验和感知锚点，显著提升长时程视频生成的运动保真度和4D重建质量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 流式自回归扩散模型虽能实现实时长时程视频生成，但其训练目标只优化局部帧预测，忽略世界几何与动力学一致性，导致长滚动中几何漂移累积并退化为静态或非自然运动。现有双向方法引入基于3D高斯泼溅重建的奖励信号，但单一刚体3D重建无法建模动态场景，会惩罚真实物体运动，且可通过冻结视频取巧，在自回归设置下尤其有害。

Method: 1) 用前馈4D重建奖励替代静态3D批评器，显式建模场景动力学，使连贯运动获得高一致性奖励；2) 增加运动先验，奖励自然的场景流幅度并惩罚抖动与非刚性伪影；3) 将两项与轻量感知锚点结合形成最终训练配方。

Result: 在多种自回归视频骨干及不同生成时长上，Stream4D提升了4D重建质量、更有效地保持运动，并获得更高的人类对齐偏好。

Conclusion: Stream4D验证了显式4D动态建模奖励对自回归视频生成的重要性，为长时程、实时视频生成提供了更稳健的优化信号，克服了静态重建批评器导致的运动退化与捷径问题。

Abstract: Streaming autoregressive diffusion models enable real-time, long-horizon video generation, but their training objectives optimize local frame prediction rather than the geometry and dynamics of a coherent world: long rollouts accumulate geometric drift and degrade into static or unnatural motion. Recent bidirectional approaches address this problem using rewards signals built upon 3D Gaussian-Splatting reconstruction. However, a single rigid 3d reconstruction cannot model a dynamic scene, so this critic penalizes genuine object motion as reconstruction error and is maximized by freezing the video. This shortcut is especially detrimental in the AR setting, where each chunk can propagate an already-static configuration. In this work, we propose Stream4D, which replaces the static critic with a feed-forward 4D reconstruction reward that explicitly models scene dynamics, allowing coherent motion to receive high consistency rewards. To further guide motion magnitude and quality, we add a motion prior that rewards natural scene-flow magnitude while penalizing jitter and non-rigid artifacts. Our final recipe combines these two terms with a lightweight perceptual anchor. Across various autoregressive video backbones and various generation horizons, Stream4D improves 4D reconstruction quality, preserves motion more effectively, and achieves higher human-aligned preference. Project page: https://banyuanhao.github.io/Stream4D/

### [2] [VGI-BENCH: Probing Visual Intelligence in Video Generation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19583)
*Xuan He,Cong Wei,Yuhao Cheng,Linrui Ma,Yuxuan Zhang,Zuojun Li,Yuhao Wen,Zeyi Liu,Yuren Hao,Songcheng Cai,Keming Wu,Penghui Du,Kai Zou,Rui Yang,Chenkai Sun,Ke Yang,Ping Nie,Kelsey R Allen,Chenglong Wang,Michel Galley,Jianfeng Gao,ChengXiang Zhai*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出VGI-bench基准，包含27个任务和810个实例，用于评估视频生成模型的零样本视觉推理能力。评估发现当前最强模型Seedance 2.0仅达到51.0%准确率，系统远未可靠。进一步分析揭示了输出失败模式、输入条件敏感性、合成微调的性能迁移边界，以及内部去噪过程中的有限自我修正问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 虽然视频生成模型展现出一定的零样本视觉推理能力，但可靠评估面临挑战：现有基准需要与当前视频模型的视觉先验对齐的输入、需要有效的演变过程而非仅合理的最终状态，以及需要校准任务难度使其既有挑战性又部分可行。缺少这样一个系统化的评估基准。

Method: 构建VGI-bench基准，包含27个任务和810个实例，采用两级分类体系（任务领域和技能标签）进行细粒度评估。评估当前生成系统并分析输出失败模式、输入条件敏感性、从合成微调的性能迁移边界，以及从内部去噪视角分析自我修正能力。

Result: 当前生成系统只能解决一部分基于视觉的推理任务，距离可靠仍有很大差距。最强模型Seedance 2.0仅达到51.0%准确率。内部去噪分析显示模型存在有限的自我修正能力，后期步骤主要细化早期假设而非纠正推理错误。

Conclusion: VGI-bench为视频生成模型的视觉推理能力提供了系统化评估框架，揭示了当前模型的显著局限性，旨在激发下一代视频生成模型的发展。代码和数据将公开发布。

Abstract: Recent studies suggest that video generation models can exhibit certain forms of zero-shot visual reasoning through generated frames. Yet reliable evaluation remains challenging: benchmarks should adopt inputs aligned with the visual priors of current video models, require valid evolving processes rather than only plausible final states, and calibrate task difficulty to remain challenging yet partly feasible. To this end, we introduce VGI-bench, containing 27 tasks and 810 instances, organized by a two-level taxonomy of task domains and skill tags for fine-grained evaluation of visual reasoning capabilities of video generation models. Our evaluations show that current generative systems can solve a subset of visually grounded reasoning tasks, but remain far from reliable, with even the strongest model, Seedance~2.0, achieving only 51.0% under our evaluation criteria. Our analysis further explore the output failure modes, input condition sensitivity, performance transfer boundary from synthetic fine-tuning, and internal denoising perspective revealing limited self-correction, where later steps mainly refine early hypotheses rather than correct reasoning errors. We hope VGI-bench will help stimulate the development of next-generation video generation models. We will release our code and data.

### [3] [TempJail: Temporal Jailbreak Attack against Large Vision-Language Models via Subtitle Scheduling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19737)
*Ling Zhou,Yihao Huang,Jingling Sun,Zhiwen Tian,Yi Zeng,Qihe Liu,Shijie Zhou*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出TempJail，一种针对大型视觉语言模型（LVLMs）的黑盒视频越狱框架。通过利用视频中字幕的时间调度（包括时长和时隙分配），构造与查询对齐的对话式字幕序列，从而利用LVLMs在时间维度上的脆弱性来实现越狱攻击。实验表明该方法在多个模型和数据集上显著优于现有基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的视频越狱方法主要操控视频中嵌入的文本内容，却忽略了信息在时间上的组织方式。作者发现越狱效果不仅取决于文本语义，还取决于时间呈现方式（时长和时隙分配），因此需要探索利用时间维度进行攻击的新方法。

Method: TempJail框架包括两个核心步骤：(1) 构造与查询对齐的对话式字幕序列；(2) 优化字幕的时间调度（duration and timing-slot allocation）以利用LVLMs的时间脆弱性。字幕作为攻击媒介，因为它在真实视频中常见且能在精确的时间控制下呈现语义内容而不引人注目。

Result: 在四个代表性LVLMs和两个数据集上的大量实验表明，TempJail在所有评估的模型-数据集设置中均取得了最高的攻击成功率。与最强基线相比，在GPT-5上平均ASR提升53个百分点，在Gemini 3.5-Flash上提升18个百分点。

Conclusion: 视频越狱的效果不仅取决于文本语义，还取决于信息的时间呈现方式。TempJail通过操控字幕的时间调度成功暴露了LVLMs在视频理解中的时间维度的安全漏洞，为视频越狱研究提供了新的思路。

Abstract: Large vision-language models (LVLMs) have achieved remarkable progress in video understanding and reasoning. Despite extensive studies on text- and image-based jailbreaks, video jailbreaks against LVLMs remain largely unexplored. Existing video jailbreak methods mainly manipulate textual content embedded in videos, while overlooking how such information is organized over time. Our analysis reveals that jailbreak effectiveness depends not only on the semantics of textual information but also on its temporal presentation, including duration and timing-slot allocation. Motivated by this finding, we use subtitles, which are common in real-world videos and allow semantic content to be presented under precise temporal control without appearing visually intrusive, as a natural attack medium. Based on this insight, we propose TempJail, a black-box video-based jailbreak framework that constructs query-aligned dialogue-style subtitle sequences and optimizes their temporal scheduling to exploit temporal vulnerabilities in LVLMs and elicit responses that satisfy the harmful intent of the source query. Extensive experiments on four representative LVLMs and two datasets demonstrate that TempJail achieves the highest attack success rate across all evaluated model--dataset settings, outperforming the strongest baseline by 53 and 18 percentage points in dataset-averaged ASR on GPT-5 and Gemini 3.5-Flash, respectively.

### [4] [Unwarping the Lens: A Physics-Grounded Approach to Video Glasses Removal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20212)
*Radim Spetlik,David Futschik,Radek Danecek,Feitong Tan,Ziqian Bai,Rohit Pandey,Yinda Zhang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种新的眼镜移除框架，通过从商业生成模型提取高保真合成面部图像，经过三阶段结构过滤和基于物理的镜头光学模拟训练，将其知识转移到专用的JFSnet联合特征-空间网络中，实现了高保真、结构准确的视频眼镜移除，推理速度达27.68 FPS，在感知研究中优于扩散和GAN基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统眼镜移除方法依赖大规模生成先验进行静态图像修复，但缺乏结构约束，导致身份漂移（identity drift），在静态图像和动态视频序列中均无法保持身份、表情和姿态的一致性。此外，眼镜造成的复杂折射畸变和视角相关的镜面反射遮挡了底层面部几何结构，使该任务极具挑战。

Method: 提出的转移框架包含三个关键步骤：(1) 从商业级生成模型（Nano Banana, Gemini 3 Pro Image）提取高保真合成面部图像；(2) 通过三阶段结构滤波过程对合成图像进行正则化，保持身份、表情和姿态；(3) 在训练期间应用基于物理的镜头光学模拟以提供多样化的配对数据。最终将这些知识转移到JFSnet（Joint Feature-Spatial network）中，该网络集成DINOv2语义特征与卷积解码器进行空间重建，利用平移等变约束提升时间一致性和高频细节保留。

Result: 在FFHQ子集（12,163张图像）上展示了高保真度和结构准确性，推理速度达到27.68 FPS。在CelebV-Text视频序列的感知研究中，该方法在眼部一致性、时间稳定性和整体恢复质量方面始终优于扩散和GAN基线方法。

Conclusion: 通过将生成先验的多样知识以受控方式转移到专用恢复架构中，有效解决了生成先验的随机性问题，在保持身份、表情和姿态的同时实现了高保真的视频眼镜移除，兼顾了质量与推理效率。

Abstract: High-fidelity removal of eyeglasses from video is a major challenge in facial attribute editing, as the underlying facial geometry is often obscured by complex refractive distortions and view-dependent specular reflections. While large-scale generative priors have shown promise in eye-glasses removal via static image inpainting, they often lack the structural constraints necessary to maintain identity, expression, and pose, leading to visible "identity drift" in both static images and dynamic sequences. In this paper, we propose a novel transfer framework that addresses the stochastic nature of generative priors. Our pipeline first extracts high-fidelity synthetic face images from a commercial-grade generative model (Nano Banana, Gemini 3 Pro Image), regularizes them via a three-stage structural filtering process to preserve identity, expression, and pose, and finally applies physically-based simulation of lens optics during training to provide diverse, paired data. This process transfers Nano Banana's photo-realistic, multi-view knowledge into a specialized restoration architecture, JFSnet (Joint Feature-Spatial network). JFSnet integrates DINOv2-based semantic features with a convolutional decoder for spatial reconstruction, leveraging translation equivariance constraints to improve temporal consistency and high-frequency detail preservation. Evaluations on the curated Flickr-Faces-HQ (FFHQ) subset (12,163 images) show that our approach achieves high fidelity and structural accuracy, while maintaining inference speed of 27.68 FPS. In perceptual studies on CelebV-Text video sequences, our results are consistently preferred over diffusion and GAN-based baselines for ocular consistency, temporal stability, and overall restoration quality.

### [5] [Ultra-High-Definition Restoration Transformers with Correlation Matching Transformation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20263)
*Cong Wang,Liyan Wang,Jinshan Pan,Wei Wang,Wenqi Ren,Jun Liu,Xiaochun Cao*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出UHDformer++，一个通用的基于Transformer的框架，通过四个协调的学习空间（高分辨率、低分辨率、超分辨率和低-高融合重建）解决多种超高清图像恢复任务，模型参数减少至少86%且性能显著提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 超高清（UHD）图像恢复任务（如低光照增强、去雾、去模糊、去雨、去雪）面临高分辨率带来的巨大计算负担和现有方法难以同时处理多种任务的挑战，亟需一个通用且高效的框架。

Method: UHDformer++在四个协调空间中操作：1) HR空间用于多级特征提取；2) LR空间学习紧凑代表性特征；3) SR空间对LR特征进行上采样；4) LHFR空间用于最终图像恢复。引入两个模块：FR-CMT模块通过融合最大池化和平均池化的高分辨率特征选择top C/r通道替换LR Transformer中信息量较少的通道；ACM模块自适应重新校准多尺度高分辨率特征，确保仅任务相关信息传播到LR。

Result: 与近期最先进方法相比，模型参数减少至少86%，在5种UHD恢复任务（低光增强、去雾、去模糊、去雨、去雪）上均获得显著的性能提升。

Conclusion: UHDformer++验证了多空间协调学习策略在超高清图像恢复任务中的有效性，以极低的参数开销实现了更优的性能，为UHD图像恢复提供了一个通用而高效的解决方案。

Abstract: We propose UHDformer++, a general Transformer-based framework to solve numerous Ultra-High-Definition (UHD) image restoration tasks. UHDformer++ operates across $4$ coordinated learning spaces: 1) a high-resolution space (HR) for multi-level feature extraction, 2) a low-resolution space (LR) for learning compact, representative features, 3) a super-resolution space (SR) for upsampling low-resolution features from SR, and 4) a low-high fusion and reconstruction space (LHFR) for final image restoration. Specifically, HR extracts multi-scale high-resolution features and fuses them with low-resolution cues to produce residual images, while LR distills complementary representations from HR to improve restoration quality. To supply LHFR with richer features, SR super-resolves LR outputs before fusion. We further introduce two modules to bridge the high- and low-resolution spaces. The Feature-Refined Correlation Matching Transformation (FR-CMT) module selects the top $C/r~(C~\text{denotes the number of channels;~}r\geq1~\text{controls the squeezing level})$, from the fusion between max- and mean-pooled high-resolution features to replace less informative channels in the low-resolution Transformer. The Adaptive Channel Modulator (ACM) adaptively recalibrates multi-scale high-resolution features, ensuring that only task-relevant information propagates to LR. Extensive experiments demonstrate that UHDformer++ reduces model parameters by at least 86\% compared with recent state-of-the-art methods while achieving substantial performance gains across $5$ UHD restoration tasks, including low-light image enhancement, dehazing, deblurring, deraining, and desnowing. Code will be released at https://github.com/supersupercong/uhdformerplus.
