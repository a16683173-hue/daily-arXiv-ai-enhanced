### [1] [Closing the Loop: Training-Free Revisit Consistency for Autoregressive Generative Rendering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21848)
*Wenchao Ma,Changran Liu,Sharon X. Huang,Haomiao Jiang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种无需后训练的方法，利用3D引擎提供的时序与空间对应关系，将历史潜变量块重新注入到KV缓存中，以解决条件视频生成模型在闭环轨迹中的重访不一致性问题，在保持视频质量的同时显著提升了重访一致性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有条件视频生成模型在长时序自回归生成中，当相机重访之前已超出KV缓存上下文的场景时，会生成不一致的外观（即重访不一致问题），而条件渲染图（如深度图）与几何结构仍保持完美对齐。

Method: 利用3D引擎提供的时序对应（将位姿匹配的历史潜变量块作为闭环记忆检索到KV缓存中）和空间对应（通过相机位姿和深度重投影将注意力偏向检索块中几何对应的区域）来解决重访不一致问题。

Result: 在TartanAir和TartanGround数据集的闭环轨迹上，该方法在重访一致性上优于现有无训练基线方法，且不降低整体视频质量。

Conclusion: 利用3D引擎固有的几何信息（时序和空间对应）可以有效解决视频生成模型的重访不一致问题，且无需后训练，具有实用价值。

Abstract: Recent conditional video generation models have shown promising potentials to transform 3D engine renderings, such as depth maps and untextured geometry, into photorealistic videos for gaming and immersive content creation. These applications require long-horizon auto-regressive generation that continuously synthesizes new frames while preserving a persistent 3D world. Auto-regressive generators synthesize video chunk by chunk with a bounded KV cache, so when the camera revisits a location after its context has been evicted, the model often regenerates inconsistent appearance, even though the conditioning renderings (e.g., depth) remain perfectly aligned with the underlying geometry.We address this revisit inconsistency without any post-training by exploiting correspondences the 3D engine already provides: temporal correspondence retrieves pose-matched historical latent chunks into the KV cache as loop-closure memory, while spatial correspondence from camera pose and depth reprojection biases token-level attention toward geometrically corresponding regions of the retrieved chunks. We demonstrate our method on loop-closure trajectories mined from TartanAir and TartanGround dataset to mirror complicate real-world application scenarios, where it outperforms existing training-free baselines on revisit consistency without losing overall video quality. Project Page: https://wenchao-m.github.io/ClosetheLoop.github.io/

### [2] [TRaM-VSR: Importance-Aware Token Routing and Merging for One-Step Diffusion Video Super-Resolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22231)
*Sicheng Gao,Zhuyun Zhou,Yixuan Liu,Tong Shen,Zongwei Wu,Radu Timofte*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出TRaM-VSR框架，通过Token路由与合并机制在扩散Transformer中自适应分配token，兼顾推理效率与视频超分辨率重建质量及时序一致性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有基于扩散Transformer的VSR方法因处理密集时空token序列导致计算成本二次增长，而简化方法又面临细节丢失和时序闪烁问题，尤其在单步扩散模型中更为突出。

Method: 1) 融合运动时序线索与语义文本相似度估计token重要性；2) 离线规划器校准重要性并指导token在最优分组的网络块间路由；3) 高保真局部流处理关键token，紧凑全局流聚合次要token，两者均受网络深度调制并适配扩散模型的多粒度特性。

Result: TRaM-VSR显著加速推理，同时保持SOTA级重建质量与鲁棒的时序一致性。

Conclusion: 通过Token路由与合并的自适应分配策略，可在不牺牲质量的前提下大幅提升扩散模型在VSR任务中的推理效率。

Abstract: Video super-resolution (VSR) using large-scale Diffusion Transformer (DiT) priors achieves exceptional perceptual quality but is often impractical due to the quadratic computational cost of processing dense spatio-temporal token sequences. Existing efficiency-oriented methods risk irreversible detail loss and temporal flickering, a vulnerability especially pronounced in one-step diffusion models. To address this, we propose TRaM-VSR, a Token Routing and Merging framework for adaptive token allocation, leveraging both context-aware video priors and network-level priors. First, token importance is estimated by fusing motion-sensitive temporal cues with semantic text similarity, isolating dynamic objects and structural boundaries. Next, this importance is further calibrated and adjusted by an offline planner to guide routing across optimally grouped network blocks. Technically, within each routed group, structurally critical tokens are processed in a high-fidelity local stream, while less informative tokens are aggregated into a compact global stream, both modulated by network depth and aligned with the multigranular nature of diffusion models. Extensive experiments show that TRaM-VSR accelerates inference significantly while preserving state-of-the-art reconstruction quality and robust temporal consistency. The code is available at https://github.com/Ree1s/TRaM-VSR.

### [3] [AgentHOI: Multi-Agent Reasoning for Human-Object-Interaction Video Generation via Implicit Representation Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22241)
*Ziyao Huang,Shunkai Li,Juan Cao,Chenyu Li,Youliang Zhang,Zixiang Zhou,Cong Wang,Yuan Zhou,Qinglin Lu,Fan Tang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: AgentHOI 提出了一种"先思考再生成"的文本驱动人-物交互视频生成框架，通过多智能体推理进行感知、交互和运动规划，并引入隐式文本-运动对齐策略，无需显式运动输入即可生成自然的人-物交互视频。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的人-物交互视频生成方法过度依赖显式的运动控制，导致在不同物体和交互场景下的可扩展性和泛化能力受限，难以从高层次文本意图到物理执行之间建立有效桥梁。

Method: 提出 AgentHOI 框架：(1) 采用"先思考再生成"范式，通过多智能体推理实现感知、交互和运动规划；(2) 引入隐式文本-运动对齐策略，将文本到运动的先验知识蒸馏到视频扩散模型中，在推理时无需显式运动输入。

Result: 在穿戴、骑行等具有挑战性的以物体为中心的场景中，AgentHOI 显著提升了交互自然度、物体外观保持度以及对复杂文本指令的遵循能力。

Conclusion: AgentHOI 通过多智能体推理计划和隐式文本-运动对齐，有效解决了文本驱动人-物交互视频生成中缺乏显式运动输入时的生成质量问题，展示了更好的泛化性和可控性。

Abstract: Recent advances in video diffusion models have spurred interest in human-object interaction (HOI) video generation, which demands fine-grained control over interaction logic beyond single-subject animation. However, existing HOI methods rely heavily on explicit motion control, limiting scalability and generalization across diverse objects and interactions. In this study, we propose AgentHOI, a text-driven HOI video generation following a thinking-before-generation framework that bridges the gap between high-level textual intent and physical execution through multi-agent reasoning over perception, interaction, and motion planning. Building upon the generated interaction plans, we further strengthen text-driven motion understanding. We introduce an implicit text-motion alignment strategy that distills text-to-motion priors into the video diffusion model, enabling robust HOI synthesis without explicit motion inputs at inference. Experiments show that AgentHOI significantly improves interaction naturalness, object appearance preservation, and adherence to complex textual instructions across challenging object-centric scenarios such as wearing and riding. The code is available at https://github.com/bone-11/agenthoi.

### [4] [IR275K: A Benchmark for Infrared Multi-Frame Super-Resolution Toward Efficient Remote Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22380)
*Jie Deng,Heyang Wang,Changxin Wang,Junkai Shen,Hongyi Chen,Zhiping He,Hongxing Qi,Xudong Zhang,Jianyu Wang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出IR275K——一个包含594个红外视频序列、共275,196帧的基准数据集，用于红外多帧超分辨率(MFSR)的标准化评估。同时提出轻量级状态空间模型CGMamba（10.90M参数），通过2D旋转位置编码(2D RoPE)和中心引导交叉Mamba融合实现X4超分，在红外条件下达到33.19dB PSNR。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 红外遥感中多帧超分辨率(MFSR)的评估分散于私有数据集和临时协议中，现有基准未能明确捕捉红外视频特有的热对比度、传感器噪声、弱纹理和平台引起的帧间变化。

Method: 构建IR275K基准（594序列，275,196帧），提供序列级训练/验证/测试划分和可复现的X4评估协议。提出CGMamba模型（10.90M参数，112.14G FLOPs），其核心创新为结合2D旋转位置编码(2D RoPE)与中心引导交叉Mamba(CGCM)融合机制，实现隐式多帧重建。

Result: CGMamba达到33.19dB PSNR，以显著更低计算成本超越红外单帧超分基线0.35-0.52dB。消融实验显示去除2D RoPE导致1.53dB下降和严重网格伪影，表明空间锚定对SSM跨帧门控至关重要。

Conclusion: IR275K为红外MFSR方法的精度-效率评估提供了可复现基础，CGMamba为资源受限红外传感下的空间感知SSM设计提供了具体起点。

Abstract: Efficient processing is becoming increasingly important in infrared remote sensing, where satellite constellations produce large volumes of observations under constrained detector resolution, power, and downlink bandwidth. Multi-frame super-resolution (MFSR) offers a software-based route to spatial enhancement, but its evaluation in infrared sensing remains fragmented across private datasets and ad-hoc protocols. Existing benchmarks do not explicitly capture the thermal contrast, sensor noise, weak texture, and platform-induced frame-to-frame variation that characterize infrared video. We introduce IR275K, a curated benchmark containing 594 infrared video sequences and 275,196 frames. It provides sequence-level train/validation/test splits and a reproducible X4 evaluation protocol. As an initial architectural probe, we further evaluate CGMamba, a lightweight state-space model with 10.90M parameters and 112.14G FLOPs. CGMamba combines 2D rotary position encoding (2D~RoPE) with center-guided cross-Mamba (CGCM) fusion for implicit multi-frame reconstruction. It achieves 33.19dB PSNR, outperforming infrared single-image super-resolution references by 0.35--0.52~dB at substantially lower computational cost. Ablation results show that removing 2D~RoPE from CGCM causes a 1.53dB drop and severe grid-like artifacts. This indicates that explicit spatial anchoring is critical for stabilizing SSM-based cross-frame gating under infrared conditions. IR275K provides a reproducible foundation for accuracy--efficiency evaluation of infrared MFSR methods, while the architectural analysis offers a concrete starting point for spatially aware SSM design under resource-constrained infrared sensing. Dataset and evaluation resources are available at: https://github.com/InfraRecon7/IR275K.
