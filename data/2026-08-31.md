### [1] [DensityKV: Density-Guided KV Cache Compression for Long Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27922)
*Wenqu Zhao,Xuemin Chi,Xin Zhang,Guoqing Ma,Baorun Li,Jianjie Fang,Peizhi Tang,Chen Gao,Wei Wu*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: DensityKV是一种无需训练的历史KV存储管理策略，通过Soft-Riesz密度度量来限制历史状态的冗余累积，在固定历史KV容量下提升自回归视频生成的长期一致性和稳定性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自回归视频扩散模型通过滑动窗口注意力实现流式生成，但每个生成块都依赖于之前生成的内容，导致外观和运动误差随时间递归传播。历史KV记忆虽然能保持长期一致性，但保留所有生成状态会使历史存储无限增长，且递归状态会反复增加冗余覆盖。

Method: DensityKV为每个注意力头维护独立的token级KV存储，使用Soft-Riesz密度度量post-RoPE键的局部冗余性。通过约束状态进入存储后的邻域密度增长，限制重复历史累积，同时保留每个完成生成块中的连贯状态。

Result: 在三个自回归视频生成骨干网络和多种生成长度上的实验表明，在相同历史KV容量上限下，DensityKV改善了长期一致性和生成稳定性，同时使持久历史存储量独立于 rollout长度保持有界。

Conclusion: DensityKV提供了一种有效的训练无关方案来解决自回归视频生成中历史KV存储无限增长和冗余累积的问题，在有限容量下实现了更好的长期一致性和稳定性。

Abstract: Autoregressive video diffusion models enable streaming generation through sliding-window attention, but each generated block is conditioned on previously generated content, causing appearance and motion errors to propagate recursively over time. Historical key-value (KV) memory preserves earlier subject and scene states and helps maintain long-horizon consistency. However, retaining every generated state creates a historical archive that grows continuously with the rollout, while recurrent states repeatedly add redundant coverage. To address this problem, we propose DensityKV, a training-free historical KV bank management strategy. DensityKV maintains a separate token-level KV bank for each attention head and measures local redundancy among the post-RoPE keys that directly parameterize attention routing using Soft-Riesz density. By constraining neighborhood-density growth after states enter the bank, DensityKV limits repeated historical accumulation while preserving coherent states from each completed generation block. Experiments across three autoregressive video generation backbones and multiple generation lengths show that, at the same upper bound on historical KV capacity, DensityKV improves long-horizon consistency and generation stability while keeping persistent historical storage bounded independently of rollout length.

### [2] [GAN-Based Semantic Communication for Image Transmission in IoV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27989)
*Ruixing Ren,Shan Chen,Junhui Zhao,Xiaoke Sun*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于生成对抗网络的语义通信框架用于车联网协同感知，通过金字塔注意力网络提取语义标签图、语义优先级保留机制分配差异化权重、以及粗到细多分辨率生成器和多尺度判别器实现高保真图像重建，在Cityscapes数据集上优于现有方法，并在AWGN和瑞利信道下保持稳定性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统通信系统在有限带宽和动态信道条件下传输视觉数据时存在效率低下和保真度不足的瓶颈，亟需更高效的语义通信方案以满足车联网协同感知需求。

Method: 提出GAN-based语义通信框架：发射端采用金字塔注意力网络提取语义标签图，并设计语义优先级保留机制，根据驾驶安全对语义类别赋予差异化权重以指导比特分配和损失函数设计；接收端设计粗到细多分辨率生成器与多尺度判别器，结合时间一致性分支、空间金字塔池化和类别感知卷积层，从受损语义标签重建高质量图像；训练采用对抗损失、特征匹配损失和感知损失的组合。

Result: 在Cityscapes数据集上，该方法在语义分割精度和重建图像质量两方面均优于现有方法，并在AWGN和瑞利信道条件下保持稳定的重建性能。

Conclusion: 所提出的语义通信框架有效解决了限制带宽下的视觉数据传输效率和保真度问题，为车联网协同感知提供了可靠的解决方案。

Abstract: For cooperative perception in the internet of vehicles, this paper proposes a generative adversarial network-based semantic communication framework to address the efficiency and fidelity bottlenecks of traditional communication systems in visual data transmission under limited bandwidth and dynamic channel conditions. At the transmitter, the framework adopts a pyramid attention network to extract semantic label maps and introduces a semantic priority preservation mechanism. It assigns differentiated weights to distinct semantic categories based on driving safety, guiding bit allocation and loss function design. At the receiver, an image reconstruction module integrating a coarse to-fine multi-resolution generator and multi-scale discriminator is designed. Combined with the temporal consistency branch, spatial pyramid pooling and class-aware convolutional layers, it achieves high-fidelity reconstruction of high-quality images from corrupted semantic labels. The model is trained with combined adversarial, feature matching and perceptual losses, effectively improving semantic consistency and visual realism of generated images. Experimental results on the Cityscapes dataset show that the proposed method outperforms existing counterparts in both semantic segmentation accuracy and reconstructed image quality, and maintains stable reconstruction performance under AWGN and Rayleigh channels.

### [3] [How Far Can 5,500 Hours of Driving Take You? A Scaling Law Analysis of Video Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28404)
*Victor Besnier,Anh-Quan Cao,Elias Ramzi,Spyros Gidaris,Tuan-Hung Vu,Andrei Bursuc,Eloi Zablocki,Matthieu Cord*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文对驾驶场景下的视频扩散模型进行了系统的规模定律（scaling-law）研究，训练了从1M到9B参数的模型族，在多达5,500小时的驾驶数据上进行了不同曝光量的训练。研究发现验证损失在模型规模和训练曝光量上都遵循一致的幂律，训练曝光量对损失的改善速率远快于模型规模，因此在有限计算预算下延长训练是最有效的提升方式；但当算力和数据充足时，计算最优缩放仍倾向于增大模型规模。基于这些定律，作者训练了一个9B参数的视频扩散模型，这是目前已知在驾驶数据上从零训练的最大视频扩散模型，在nuScenes基准上刷新了开源驾驶视频生成的最佳水平。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自动驾驶视频生成无法走网络规模的路线，因为驾驶数据采集成本高昂、受隐私约束、无法随意抓取，模型必须充分利用固定规模的数据集。因此需要系统性地回答如何配置训练预算的问题：算力应花在更长训练还是更大模型上，以及是否需要更多数据。

Method: 对从1M到9B参数的一系列视频扩散模型进行从零训练实验，在不同训练曝光量（exposure）下使用最多5,500小时驾驶数据进行训练，构建模型规模与训练数据之间的规模定律，并通过验证损失拟合幂律关系来指导计算最优的训练配置。

Result: 发现验证损失在模型规模和训练曝光量上均呈现一致的幂律行为；训练曝光量对损失的改善速度远快于模型规模。基于规模定律的指导，训练了9B参数的视频扩散模型，在nuScenes上达到了驾驶视频生成的开放源码最优水平（SOTA）。

Conclusion: 在有限计算预算下，延长训练时间比增大模型规模更有效；但当算力和数据充足时，增大模型规模仍能获得更低的渐近损失，计算最优缩放仍倾向于更大的模型。9B模型验证了规模定律在实际应用中的指导价值。

Abstract: Video generation for autonomous driving cannot follow the web-scale route: driving data is expensive to collect, bound by privacy requirements, and cannot be scraped at will, so models must make the most of a fixed corpus. We present a systematic scaling-law study of video diffusion models trained from scratch on driving data: a family of models from 1M to 9B parameters, trained at different exposures on up to 5,500 hours of driving. Validation loss follows consistent power laws in both model size and training exposure, answering the questions that shape a training budget: whether compute is better spent on longer training or on a larger model, and whether more data is needed. Loss improves much faster with training exposure than with model size, making longer training the most effective way to improve a fixed model under limited compute. However, larger models continue to achieve lower asymptotic loss, so compute-optimal scaling still favors increasing model size when sufficient compute and data are available. Guided by these laws, we train a 9B-parameter model, to our knowledge the largest video diffusion model trained from scratch on driving data: it sets a new open-source state of the art for driving video generation, as measured on nuScenes. Our code and pretrained models are available at https://github.com/valeoai/VATIX. NATIX is separately releasing the underlying driving data in stages.

### [4] [LayerRecall: A State-Conditioned Memory Router for Long-Horizon Consistency in Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28460)
*Yixuan Ding,Jiahao Kong,Wei Huang,Ruijie Quan,Yi Yang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出LayerRecall，一种用于自回归视频扩散模型的层选择性记忆路由机制，通过当前条件引导检索相关历史K/V状态，并仅注入到记忆敏感层的骨干网络，配合Cross-Horizon Prediction Matching（CHPM）训练策略，在不需要高质量长视频和显式记忆分配标签的情况下实现长距离语义恢复，同时保持局部连续性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自回归视频扩散模型通过有界近期上下文生成视频块，虽然保持了局部连续性，但当主体、物体、场景或属性重新出现时会丢失历史线索。现有记忆机制虽然让模型接触到非局部历史，但仅仅接触并不能确保有效使用。分析发现视频DiT层对当前、近期和远期上下文有不同的偏好，说明长距离记忆需要同时决定检索什么和在哪里使用。

Method: 1) LayerRecall：当前条件化的层选择性记忆路由器，检索相关历史K/V状态，仅注入到骨干网络中对记忆敏感的层，同时保留其余层的局部注意力。2) CHPM（Cross-Horizon Prediction Matching）：利用特权长上下文参考在预测空间监督有界记忆路由器，减少对稀缺高质量长视频和显式记忆分配标签的依赖。

Result: 在100个多镜头评估提示中，LayerRecall在MemoBench和MovieBench上取得最佳总体结果，同时在VBench-Long上与骨干网络持平。定性分析显示记忆引导的自我修正能力，附加分析显示跨骨干可移植性和可忽略的推理开销。

Conclusion: LayerRecall证明了在自回归视频生成中，层选择性记忆注入是恢复长距离语义且不牺牲局部连续性的有效方法，其自我修正能力表明记忆引导可以动态纠正局部属性偏差而不中断进行中的运动或场景结构。

Abstract: Autoregressive video diffusion enables scalable long-video generation by producing chunks from a bounded recent context. While recency-based caching preserves local continuity, it evicts historical cues needed when subjects, objects, scenes, or attributes reappear. Existing memory mechanisms expose models to nonlocal history, but access alone does not ensure effective use. Our analysis reveals that video DiT layers exhibit distinct preferences for current, recent, and distant context, suggesting that long-range memory requires deciding both what to retrieve and where to use it. We introduce LayerRecall, a current-conditioned, layer-selective memory router that retrieves relevant historical K/V states and injects them only into backbone-specific memory-sensitive layers while preserving local attention elsewhere. To reduce reliance on scarce high-quality long-horizon videos and explicit memory-allocation labels, we further propose Cross-Horizon Prediction Matching (CHPM), which uses a privileged long-context reference to supervise the bounded-memory router in prediction space. Across 100 multi-shot evaluation prompts, LayerRecall achieves the best overall results on MemoBench and MovieBench while matching its backbone on VBench-Long, demonstrating stronger long-range recovery without sacrificing local continuity. Qualitative analyses further reveal memory-guided self-correction, whereby initially mismatched local attributes return to their historical appearance without resetting ongoing motion or scene structure. Additional analyses show cross-backbone portability and negligible inference overhead.

</details>

### [5] [Fully Unleashing the Multimodal Attacker: Meta-Adaptive Jailbreaking of Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27531)
*Benlei Cui,Shen Pang,Yuke Wang,Xuemei Dong,Yuwen Zhai,Jingqun Tang,Haiyang Yu,Hui Xue,Longtao Huang,Haiwen Hong*

Main category: cs.CR

TL;DR: 本文提出Meta-Adaptive Multimodal Jailbreaking (MAMJ)，一种元自适应多模态越狱攻击方法。与现有静态模板或仅调整内容的攻击不同，MAMJ同时优化攻击策略提示词θ和攻击者权重φ，通过LLM批评和组聚合的ASR奖励进行迭代更新。在MM-SafetyBench上对GPT-4o、Gemini-3-Pro-Preview和Seed 2.0分别达到81.0%、78.9%和82.3%的越狱成功率，超越最强基线24.1个百分点，且学到的攻击者可以无需重训练迁移到未见受害者。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的多模态越狱攻击在元层面是静态的：模板攻击冻结了图文布局，迭代攻击虽自适应内容但攻击策略和参数固定。这种静态性限制了攻击的适应性和威胁程度，因此需要对攻击者本身进行元层面的优化。

Method: MAMJ在两个维度上优化攻击者：(1) 攻击策略提示词(ASP) θ控制攻击迭代过程，由LLM-based批评机制在多模态攻击轨迹组中精炼；(2) 攻击者权重φ决定攻击有效性，通过组聚合的攻击成功率(ASR)奖励更新。整体采用"批评-精炼-更新"的元级循环。

Result: 在MM-SafetyBench基准上，MAMJ对GPT-4o、Gemini-3-Pro-Preview和Seed 2.0分别达到81.0%、78.9%和82.3%的ASR，比最强样本级基线提升最多24.1个百分点。学到的攻击者(θ*,φ*)在无需重训练的情况下可迁移到未见受害者，并且在代表性防御下仍保持有效。

Conclusion: MAMJ揭示了大视觉语言模型对元自适应越狱存在系统性漏洞，这些攻击者在元层面进化，超越了内容层面的静态攻击范式。研究结果呼吁防御策略需要针对元级别对手进行设计，而不仅仅是处理内容层面的攻击。

Abstract: The safety of large vision-language models is increasingly stress-tested by multimodal jailbreaks, yet existing attacks remain largely static at the meta level: template-based attacks freeze the image--text layout, while iterative attacks adapt only the image--text content with fixed attack strategies and frozen attacker parameters. We propose Meta-Adaptive Multimodal Jailbreaking (MAMJ), which instead optimizes the attacker itself along two axes: an attack strategy prompt (ASP) $θ$ governing attack iteration and attacker weights $φ$ determining attack effectiveness. Across groups of multimodal attack trajectories, an LLM-based critique first refines $θ$, after which group-aggregated attack-success-rate (ASR) rewards update $φ$. On MM-SafetyBench, MAMJ achieves $81.0\%$, $78.9\%$, and $82.3\%$ ASR against GPT-4o, Gemini-3-Pro-Preview, and Seed 2.0, respectively, outperforming the strongest sample-level baseline by up to $24.1$ percentage points. The learned attacker $(θ^\star,φ^\star)$ also transfers without retraining to unseen victims and remains effective under representative defenses. These results reveal a systemic vulnerability of frontier VLMs to meta-adaptive jailbreaks and motivate defenses against meta-level adversaries. Code is available at https://github.com/Alibaba-VELLDEPTH/MetaJailbreak-VLM.

</details>
